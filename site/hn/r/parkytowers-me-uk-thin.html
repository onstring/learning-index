  • If you just want a little Linux box and don't need GPIO, I'd recommend buying a used "thin client" from eBay. These are usually low-end x86(-64) machines with decent connectivity but not much storage, as they're meant to just display the UI for apps running on a server somewhere. You can generally hack more storage into them pretty easily. A 4-thread machine with case, heat sink, power supply, 8gb ram, and an SSD can be had for less than the MSRP of a bare 8GB RPi 4.
