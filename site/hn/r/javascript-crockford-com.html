  • If you have a cursory understanding of JavaScript and it's syntax i highly recommend you check out some of Douglas Crockford's content: http://javascript.crockford.com/ . He is the man who invented the JSON format (if you don't know what that is yet, don't worry, you'll come across it soon enough), and wrote an excellent tool called JSLint.
