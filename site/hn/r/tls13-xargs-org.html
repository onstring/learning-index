  • This is a great explanation, and the point about TLS is well taken as well. If you want to go for that level of misdirection, then depending on your threat model you might consider e.g. using a remote proxy and wrapping your session to it in TLS. The tricky thing about baking TLS in at the protocol level is that it brings its baggage with it. This is not necessarily a bad thing, but it makes the design more complex to reason about. In particular, it is arguably overkill when you're not planning on using certificates, as is the case here.
