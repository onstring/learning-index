About

HN Learning Index lists free online learning resources that Hacker News commenters keep recommending: tutorials, courses, free books, reference documentation and explanatory posts. It is built in the spirit of hackerbooks.org and hackertube.org, but for material you can read for free in your browser.

What gets in

A resource qualifies when at least 3 different Hacker News commenters have recommended it, often years apart. Each one is then checked, automatically, to be free to read in full, to teach or explain something rather than report news or sell a product, and to be worth reading regardless of the date. Videos, code repositories and pages for paid books are left out. The same automatic step assigns a topic, a resource type and a rough difficulty level, and writes the one-sentence description.

The checks are automatic, so they are not perfect. Expect the occasional item that should not be here and the occasional good resource that is missing. Resources are ranked by how many different people recommended them and over how many years. Nothing here is paid placement.
