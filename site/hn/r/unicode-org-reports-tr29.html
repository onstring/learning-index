  • A codepoint is the smallest unit of meaning in unicode. A byte is just a number, that might (or might not) have meaning in a specific unicode encoding. (Also depending on what other bytes it's next to). A codepoint is the smallest unit that has a graphical representation you can print on screen. A codepoint is the smallest unit that allows API's that are agnostic to encoding, just in terms of the semantic content of unicode. If you want to write any kind of algorithm in terms of the actual character meaning (charecters represented), you want a codepoint abstraction.
