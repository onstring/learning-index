Unenumerated

https://unenumerated.blogspot.com · 中文 (Google 翻译)

Explores historical examples of non-governmental money systems, including privately issued bank notes and monetary metals, with a focus on their relevance to modern economic concepts.

Explainer Intermediate Recommended by 3 different commenters across 3 years (2008–2021)

Discussed on Hacker News: Some hard things are easy when explained well (1 points)

What Hacker News says

  • I highly recommend Unqualified Reservations ( http://unqualified-reservations.blogspot.com/ ) and Unenumerated ( http://unenumerated.blogspot.com/ ) Both are incredibly smart blogs, with very smart commenters. Their main shtick is bringing historical perspective to contemporary problems of government and economics. The result is fascinating and eye opening. I've learned more history from these two blogs then I learned from majoring in history at Yale.
