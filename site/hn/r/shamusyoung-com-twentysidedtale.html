Shamus Young's Twenty-Sided Tale

https://shamusyoung.com/twentysidedtale · 中文 (Google 翻译)

Explores game design, programming, and AI behavior through detailed analysis of video game mechanics and systems.

Explainer Intermediate Recommended by 4 different commenters across 4 years (2009–2023)

Discussed on Hacker News: Lost Laughs in Leisure Suit Larry (367 points)

What Hacker News says

  • >yet the entities in video games don't _act_ like humans This was the source of a lot of complaints about Cyberpunk 2077. They made a lavishly detailed world, but it was all a millimeter of paint over nothing. Food stalls that don't sell food, cars that disappear when you're not looking at them. As it turns out, graphics are a lot easier than behavior! We should have guessed this, but while you can see all the complicated visual detail that should be hard to replicate, behavior is invisible. Shamus Young had a good post drilling allllll the way down into the nitty gritty details in one small AI subsystem of a game, Thief 3, and what it would mean to upgrade it: https://www.shamusyoung.com/tw

    sbierwagen, 2021 · comment · in A sneak peek at MetaHuman Creator

  • Prior art: https://www.shamusyoung.com/twentysidedtale/?p=2124 It is genuinely a little spooky to me that we've reached a point where a specific software architecture confabulated as a plot-significant aspect of a fictional AGI in a fanfiction novel about a video game from the 90s is also something that may merit seriously consideration as a potential option for reducing AI alignment risk. (It's a great novel, though, and imo truer to System Shock's characters than the game itself was able to be. Very much worth a read, unexpectedly tangential to the topic of the moment or no.)

    throwanem, 2023 · comment · in Bing: “I will not harm you unless you harm me first”

  • I've followed Shamus Young's blog for years. His best-known writing is game story analysis like this Mass Effect series, but he also has a great gift for describing programming topics in simpler terms for non-programmers to follow. As one example, his "Pixel City" series builds up an application for procedurally generating city environments ( http://www.shamusyoung.com/twentysidedtale/?p=2940 ). I highly recommend his blog to anyone interested in gaming, programming, or just looking for a site with high-quality writing and discussion.

    acemarke, 2017 · comment · in A 50-chapter retrospective on the original Mass Effect games
