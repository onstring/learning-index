The Elements of Computing Systems

https://www.nand2tetris.org/book · 中文 (Google 翻译)

Build a computer from logic gates to applications through hands-on projects and theoretical concepts.

Free book Intermediate Recommended by 5 different commenters across 4 years (2019–2023)

What Hacker News says

  • The best courses I took in Computer Science (3 years ago) were: - Computer Systems : Building a computer system from scratch (virtually) logic gates to applications https://www.nand2tetris.org/book - Computer Networks and Applications : An overview of network protocols and layers, basically how the internet works. - Event-Driven Computing : How to make GUI's, state-machines, event-driven systems are used everywhere. - Computer Graphics : Understanding how graphics pipelines and OpenGL shaders work. Building a game from scratch in C. They might not be interesting to everyone, but I felt these were the most fun and practical in learning how computers work and how to build software.

    gitgud, 2020 · comment · in Ask HN: I'm an incoming freshman to college for a CS major, what should I know?

  • I'm not sure what you're looking for (maybe I mis-read your requirements above), but for the step from "I understand the idea of simple boolean logic" to "I understand (quite concretely) how a Z80 (or 6502 or similar) CPU works", I'd recommend the book "The Elements of Computing Systems" from the Nand2Tetris site: https://www.nand2tetris.org/book It's what made things click for me. [EDIT: I also second Klelatti's suggestion... Ben Eater's youtube channel is ALL gold. He's an amazing teacher. I also purchased his clock module, and the 6502 kit - they are awesome!]

    mrspeaker, 2020 · comment · in Z80, the 8-bit Number Cruncher (2011)

  • If learning this type of low-level CS is interesting, I can't recommend the NAND to Tetris course highly enough ( https://www.coursera.org/learn/build-a-computer ). You start with a NAND gate (NOT AND) and built the simple gates, multiplexers, ALU, machine language, VM, compiler for a high level lang, OS, graphics engine, culminating with a game you design that runs on this computer. There's also a book with the same contents ( https://www.nand2tetris.org/book ).

    jamestimmins, 2023 · comment · in A 32-Bit Computer Inside Terraria [video]
