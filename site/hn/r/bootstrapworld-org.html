Bootstrap

https://bootstrapworld.org · 中文 (Google 翻译)

Students learn algebra and data science concepts through programming video games using functional programming in Scheme.

Course Intermediate Recommended by 4 different commenters across 4 years (2010–2024)

Discussed on Hacker News: Computational Modeling in Algebra, Physics and Data Science, for All Students (110 points)

What Hacker News says

  • I taught 3 Bootstrap courses last year to middle-schoolers, with pretty good success. Bootstrap is an introduction to functional programming in Scheme. The students write simple callback functions to implement animation and collision detection in a 2D side-scroller video game framework. I think it's a pretty good introduction to programming for kids of middle school age, though it assumes familiarity with elementary algebra and the Cartesian coordinate system. Others have taught it to students in the 5th grade, who had no prior algebra experience, though I can't personally vouch for how effective it is for those students.
