Eastern Classics Reading List

https://www.sjc.edu/academic-programs/graduate/masters-eastern-classics/reading-lists · 中文 (Google 翻译)

Students explore foundational texts from Eastern philosophical and literary traditions including Confucianism, Taoism, and Indian philosophy through primary source readings.

  • The College offers a Master’s degree in Eastern Classics, applying the same approach to a literary tradition which, while it intersects with and interacts with the Western tradition, easily also stands on its own: https://www.sjc.edu/academic-programs/graduate/masters-easte... The monolithic, slow-to-change Great Books list that forms the core of the St John’s undergraduate Reading List is famously and self-consciously concerned primarily and narrowly with the Western intellectual tradition that informed the Founding Fathers, etc. - so as, in the creators’ thinking, to prepare graduates for citizenship in a liberal democracy. It’s likely no one is more critical of the List than the students
