  • You might be interested in “Eggex”, which aims to be a human-readable language that generates regexes. It’s currently written as a feature of the Oil shell, but in theory any tool could support them. Eggex docs: https://www.oilshell.org/release/latest/doc/eggex.html . Recent blog post about their development: https://www.oilshell.org/blog/2019/12/22.html . However, Eggexes are a thin, mostly-syntactic layer over regexes. You still have to understand the regex engine to use them.
