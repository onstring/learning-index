Appropriate Uses For SQLite

https://sqlite.org/whentouse.html · 中文 (Google 翻译)

Learn when to use SQLite for local data storage in embedded systems and applications, avoiding client/server database engines.

Reference Intermediate Recommended by 6 different commenters across 4 years (2018–2026)

Discussed on Hacker News: Appropriate Uses for SQLite (413 points)

What Hacker News says

  • Here's a good place to start: https://www.sqlite.org/whentouse.html https://www.sqlite.org/quirks.html Full-scale RDBMSs, especially Postgres, have lots of goodies that either SQLlite doesn't have (or which it does have, but which aren't so richly featured). Once you've gotten hooked on a few of these, the distinction will feel a lot more clear. Meanwhile the tipping points in favor of SQLite seem to be embedded systems, and its whole "service-less" architecture and plain ease of use. Which is why it still gets lots of love, for those contexts.

    lisasays, 2023 · comment · in Ask HN: It's 2023, how do you choose between MySQL and Postgres?

  • "Production" can mean many different things to different people. It's very widely used as a backend strutured file format in Android and iOS/macOS (e.g. for appls like Notes, Photos). Is that "production"? It's not widely used and largely inappropriate for applications with many concurrent writes. Sqlite docs has a good overview of appropriate and inappropriate uses: https://sqlite.org/whentouse.html It's best to start with Section 2 "Situations Where A Client/Server RDBMS May Work Better"

    em500, 2026 · comment · in Databases in 2025: A Year in Review

  • I'm working towards a 1.0 release. At that point documentation will be more complete and the plumbing needed to use it in an app should be also reduced. I think the best explanation of why you'd use it over PG or MySQL is the same as why you'd use SQLite over them: https://www.sqlite.org/whentouse.html With dqlite you also get HA, fault-tolerance and transparent failover, if you're willing to trade it with a bit of performance.

    free-ekanayaka, 2018 · comment · in Distributed SQLite for Go applications
