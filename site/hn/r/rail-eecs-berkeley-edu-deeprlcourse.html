Work through David Silver's course (search youtube, it overlaps heavily with the book above) and then you are ready for something more advanced like [2] and you can start reading and implementing research papers. [1] http://incompleteideas.net/book/the-book.html [2] http://rail.eecs.berkeley.edu/deeprlcourse/
