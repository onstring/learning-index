The Theoretical Minimum

https://theoreticalminimum.com · 中文 (Google 翻译)

Learn modern physics and cosmology through courses and books by Leonard Susskind, covering classical mechanics, relativity, and quantum mechanics.

Course Intermediate Recommended by 6 different commenters across 4 years (2018–2022)
