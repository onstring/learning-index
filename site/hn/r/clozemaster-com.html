Clozemaster

https://www.clozemaster.com/ · 中文 (Google 翻译)

Learn vocabulary through fill-in-the-blank exercises in real sentences across 50+ languages, with spaced repetition for retention.

Interactive Beginner Recommended by 3 different commenters across 2 years (2018–2023)
