Finder

https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2003/04/finder/ · 中文 (Google 翻译)

Explores the history and design philosophy of the Mac OS X Finder, contrasting it with the spatial metaphor of the Classic Mac OS Finder.

Explainer Intermediate Recommended by 5 different commenters across 4 years (2018–2024)

Discussed on Hacker News: If Mac OS X has a secret shame, it's the Finder (2003) (25 points)

What Hacker News says

  • The classic MacOS's Finder was 'spatial'. Since each folder on disk could only be open in a single window, and that window remembered its position and layout, there was a certain physical consistency - it felt like the windows were the actual folders on disk, with the same kind of coherency and stability you expect of real-world objects. Meanwhile, the Mac OS X Finder is not spatial. You can have the same folder open in multiple windows, each with different layouts. There's no longer that illusion that the window and the folder are one and the same – it's just a browser showing you the contents of the folder. This article goes into a lot more detail. https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2003/04/f
