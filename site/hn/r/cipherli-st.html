Cipherli.st

http://cipherli.st · 中文 (Google 翻译)

Learn to configure strong ciphers for web servers like Apache, Nginx, and Lighttpd with practical recommendations and rationale.

Reference Intermediate Recommended by 4 different commenters across 3 years (2015–2017)

Discussed on Hacker News: Cipherli.st – Strong Ciphers for Apache, Nginx and Lighttpd (55 points)

What Hacker News says

  • I'd recommend checking out https://cipherli.st for practical information on ciphers. It's quite easy to achieve an A+ by following their recommendations, once you understand the impact some of the changes might have (eg, using HSTS). There's even a set on config for poor sysadmins who are forced to support legacy clients on XP. Happy to answer questions as well - I do this for work.

    Daneel_, 2015 · comment · in Why your 'A' grade SSL is 'outdated cryptography' on Chrome

  • why wait? i made the switch from startcom to letsencrypt in a few hours spending most of the time updating my ssl configuration (using https://cipherli.st/ at the time). `./certbot renew` is easy to throw into a cron job, too, at least much easier than the classic startcom certificate generation/signing/etc process.

    nsfmc, 2016 · comment · in StartCom operated solely in China: an analysis of the new StartCom website

  • I love https://cipherli.st too - it's definitely worth reading their rationales - but the Mozilla Server Side TLS project the other poster and the article mentioned seems to have more contributors and be more frequently updated (it's also on github).

    nailer, 2015 · comment · in SSL tools we wish we'd known about earlier
